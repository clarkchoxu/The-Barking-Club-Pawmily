About us

We are a family-owned kennel based in Bocaue, Bulacan, Philippines, dedicated to raising and caring for beautiful companion dogs. Our passion goes beyond breeding — it is about nurturing happy, healthy, and well-cared-for furbabies who bring joy and unconditional love to their future families.

We take pride in our carefully selected breeds, beautiful appearances, exceptional bloodlines, and commitment to responsible pet care. From Toy and Teacup Poodles to Bichon Frise, Shih Tzu, Maltese, Chihuahua, and other beloved companions, each of our furbabies receives individual attention, proper care, and affection.


We specialize in premium Poodles, Shih Tzus, and other small-breed companions, proudly carrying imported and local champion bloodlines. Each puppy is raised with genuine love and care🥰 ✅a PCCI-registered and FCI International-accredited breeder

Our story

The Barking Club Pawmily is a family-owned kennel in Bocaue, Bulacan, Philippines. We are dedicated to raising and caring for companion dogs with love and attention.
